# Official New Pokémon Snap promotional images

These three images were requested by the project owner for the launcher and
downloaded directly from the official [New Pokémon Snap website](https://newpokemonsnap.pokemon.com/en-us/)
on **2026-11-14 UTC**. Their original response bytes are preserved: no crop,
resize, recolouring, re-encoding or image generation was performed.

| Local file | Official source | Native dimensions | Bytes |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `lental-hero.jpg` | [Homepage intro fallback](https://newpokemonsnap.pokemon.com/assets/img/homepage/intro-fallback.jpg) | 375 × 494 | 21,580 |
| `lental-explore.jpg` | [Homepage explore image](https://newpokemonsnap.pokemon.com/assets/img/homepage/explore.jpg) | 322 × 181 | 4,966 |
| `snap-logo.png` | [New Pokémon Snap logo](https://newpokemonsnap.pokemon.com/assets/img/logos/snap-logo.png) | 484 × 283 | 41,783 |

`source-pins.json` records exact source/final URLs, official page, UTC retrieval
timestamps, SHA256, byte sizes, formats, dimensions and validation for each file.
Downloads were bounded to 10 MiB each and 40 million decoded pixels; redirects
were restricted to HTTPS on the same official host. All three fully decode with
Pillow and were visually inspected. The logo's original palette/transparency is
preserved. The small native image sizes above are the actual supplied files,
not high-resolution replacements.

The portrait shows Lental grassland with Vivillon and Bouffalant; the landscape
shows a desert sandstorm. The PNG is the official game logo. No Kingdom Hearts
art, ROM-extracted resources, private screenshots, saved photos or owner inputs
were used.
