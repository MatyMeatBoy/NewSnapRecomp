# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.3

> **Defective above 60 FPS; replaced by 0.9.4.** The interface change described below showed hidden
> menus and made the interface flicker every frame. The measurements quoted here did not detect it.

**Stable line 0.9.** A native Windows recompilation of New Pokémon Snap 2.0.1. Bring your own game:
the launcher imports your own dump (NSP, NSZ, XCI or XCZ with the 2.0.1 update and your keys) and builds
the game locally. No game data, keys or game-derived executables are distributed.

## New in 0.9.3

- **Menus keep their speed above 60 FPS.** The game's interface advances one animation frame per drawn frame,
  so at 144 FPS every menu, popup, score screen, loading animation and button prompt ran 2.4 times faster while
  the 3D scene kept its speed. Above 60 FPS the interface now advances 60 steps a second of real time; at 60 FPS
  and below nothing changes. Measured on an RTX 4060: 60.0 interface steps a second at 144 FPS, with a steady
  picture on the frames in between. `AC_SNAP_UI_PACE_PATCH=0` turns it off. Not yet run on AMD hardware, and only
  the tutorial route was played; game logic that counts frames outside the interface was not audited.
- **Internal resolution reads as one scale** in the in-game Screen page: 50 % (lightest) up to 300 % (sharpest) with
  the game's own 100 % in the middle, then By display.
- **Automatic upscaler steps aside for supersampling.** With the upscaler on Automatic, an internal resolution above
  100 % now uses Original by itself (supersampling only runs without an upscaler). A chosen upscaler is not overridden.

## Highlights

- **Graphics presets** with **«Recomendado»** by default: resolution by display, AF 16×, shadows Auto, HD photo
  thumbnails, 60 FPS, borderless window at the desktop's size. Plus Original, Bajo, Medio, Alto and Ultra.
- **Internal resolution** from 50 % to 300 % or by display, with HUD, photos and scoring intact.
- **Upscalers:** AMD FSR 3.1 (any GPU), AMD FSR 4 (RX 6000/7000 through AMD's INT8 model, RX 9000 FP8) and NVIDIA
  DLSS (RTX), with Automatic picking the best available.
- **AMD FSR frame generation** (optional) with the interface kept out of the interpolation.
- **90, 120 and 144 FPS** options; FreeSync/G-SYNC used as the driver provides it.
- **HD photo thumbnails**, **higher-resolution shadows**, **experimental ultrawide** (photos stay 16:9).
- **«Mejoras» tab** in the launcher and **Enhancements** tab in the in-game menu (Minus + Plus) with every NewSnap
  enhancement switchable; options that cannot apply on your GPU are greyed out with the reason.
- **Shader cache kept across updates** that do not change shaders, so updating does not mean compiling again.
- **HD texture packs** (runtime part): packs by content hash, made locally from your own game.

## Requirements

Windows x64, a CPU with AVX2, a Vulkan 1.3 GPU (AMD RX 6000 or newer, NVIDIA RTX, recent Intel), your own New Pokémon
Snap 2.0.1 dump and `prod.keys`.

## Tested

Daily on an AMD RX 6900 XT and an NVIDIA RTX 4060. Known limits and open work are listed in `docs/PENDIENTES.md`.
