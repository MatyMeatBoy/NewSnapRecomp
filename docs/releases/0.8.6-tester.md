# NewSnapRecomp 0.8.6-tester

**Tester build.** Replaces 0.8.5-tester.

- **FPS «Según la pantalla» (PACE-AMD-01).** On a fixed-refresh monitor without VRR, a 60 cap
  judders: at 144 Hz each frame stays 2 or 3 refreshes (13.9/20.8 ms), most visible when turning
  the camera. The new frame-rate choice caps at the smallest exact fraction of the refresh at or
  above ~60 (144 Hz → 72, 100 Hz → 100, 120 Hz → 60, 165 Hz → 82.5) and sends each present to the
  middle of its refresh interval using Windows' vblank timing (DWM), as Special K's Latent Sync
  does, so timer jitter never moves a frame to another refresh. Zelda64Recomp's
  «Framerate: Display» is the same idea. Off by default; also in the in-game menu as
  «Auto (display)». With FreeSync/G-SYNC active, keep a normal cap instead.
- **VRR guidance** in `docs/DISPLAY.md`: the driver, not the game, turns VRR on; how to check it.
- **Ultrawide investigated, not shipped** (`docs/ULTRAWIDE.md`): widening 16:9 cameras at
  `main+0x29641c` gives a correct wider 3D view, but Snap captures photos from that same frame
  (they would come out squeezed and score differently) and the HUD stretches.
- **The whole repository's documentation is now in English** (bug reports and old release notes;
  the archived Spanish board stays as history).

Tested on the RX 6900 XT, 144 Hz: `display` runs at 72 fps with 100 % of presents 13.9 ms apart,
exit 0. The panel's own cadence was not measured (the AMD driver has no present timing): check it
