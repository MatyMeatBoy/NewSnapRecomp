# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.8

**Stable line 0.9.** Everything in 0.9.7, plus:

## New

- **HD texture packs from the launcher** (TEXPACK-01). Turn on «Guardar texturas para el pack» in the Mejoras tab and
  play: each colour texture is saved once. «Generar pack de texturas HD» then enlarges them on your PC with Real-ESRGAN
  (downloaded once from its official release) and compresses them to BC1/BC3, so a pack costs a quarter to an eighth of
  the video memory of plain pictures. The game uses them the next time they load. Turn the saving off when you are done:
  it lengthens loads a little. The pack is made from your own game and stays on your PC.

## Checked

RX 6900 XT: textures saved during the opening, 40 of them enlarged, loaded and drawn correctly in the next run.
