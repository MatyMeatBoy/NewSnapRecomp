# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.7

**Stable line 0.9.** A native Windows recompilation of New Pokémon Snap 2.0.1. Bring your own game: the launcher
imports your own dump (NSP, NSZ, XCI or XCZ with the 2.0.1 update and your keys) and builds the game locally. No game
data, keys or game-derived executables are distributed.

## Fixed

- **Dialogue skipped a line above 60 FPS** (INPUT-HZ-01, regression of 0.9.4). A press while a line was being typed
  completed it *and* went on to the next one. The game reads the controller once per frame, while its interface steps
  60 times a second; above 60 FPS the controller is now sampled once per interface step, as the game expects, so every
  part of the game sees each press once and at the same moment. It covers every menu and screen, since all of them read
  the controller through the same two functions. A press reaches the game at most 16.7 ms later.
- **The pointer moved too fast in the pause menu above 60 FPS** (FPS-02). The pointer uses the game's frame time,
  which is 0 while the game is paused, and then moved a fixed step per frame. It now uses the real frame time there, so
  it keeps its speed at any frame rate in every screen that uses it (pause menu, photo selection).
- **A freeze when an HD texture pack image was released** (latent in TEXPACK-01): the texture pack released its HD
  images while holding its own lock and waited on itself.

## New

- **HD scenery shadows (experimental, off by default).** The shadows of plants, rocks and scenery are baked into each
  level's light maps, which is why the shadow resolution option never reached them. Turn on «Sombras HD del escenario»
  in the launcher's Mejoras tab (or HD scenery shadows in the in-game Enhancements tab), play a level, press «Generar
  sombras HD», and enter the level again: its light maps are enlarged on your PC with Real-ESRGAN (downloaded once
  from its official release) on a logarithmic light scale, keeping the game's light and colour, without seams at the
  edges of the light-map islands. Directional light terms are left untouched.
- **Launcher redesign:** sidebar navigation with a Play button and status chips (data, runtime, shaders).
- **Internal resolution fitted to your display:** each step shows the height it draws at, the step matching your
  monitor is marked, and steps beyond twice your display's height are hidden.
- **VRR frame limit** («VRR»: just under the refresh rate, 138 at 144 Hz) in the launcher and the in-game menu;
  **Automatic** is the default upscaler; one vocabulary («Escalado») in the launcher; the game's console window is
  hidden; a lighter launcher package; test batch files are no longer shipped.

## Checked

On the RX 6900 XT at 144 Hz: dialogue at 90, 120 and 144 FPS gives the same complete/advance sequence as at 60 FPS,
with 100 ms and 250 ms presses; the pause-menu pointer covers the same distance at 144 FPS as at 60 FPS; HD shadows
generated for the first Florio area (549 light maps, 326 enlarged, 126 MB) load and play without artefacts or seams.
Not checked: an RTX GPU with this build, later levels with HD shadows, a VRR display.
