# NewSnapRecomp 0.8.0-tester

**Tester build, not stable.** Gathers what was tested after 0.7.1-tester on the RX 6900 XT. The
0.7.1 and earlier artifacts do not change.

## Changes since 0.7.1-tester

- **GYRO-02: a «Mods» tab in the launcher and gyro sensitivity.**
  - New `gyro_sens` setting (`AC_GYRO_SENS`): 2.5 by default, 0.1 to 10, applied live. It scales
    the turn rate before integrating it in `motion_fusion.c`, so the game reads turn, angle and
    direction already scaled. With a scale other than 1 the gravity correction is not applied, so
    the tilt does not drift back by itself.
  - The Mods tab gathers the gyro (on if the controller has one), its sensitivity and the mouse
    sensitivity, with the list of controllers that work without drivers (Pro, Joy-Con, DS4,
    DualSense and 8BitDo in Switch mode).
- **PIPE-MO-01: the per-object motion pass pipelines are precompiled at start-up.** Before, they
  were always discarded (99 of 286 records on the RX 6900 XT) and created during play, where RDNA
  1/2 without pipeline libraries waits or skips the draw.
- **PRESENT-WIN-01: in a window, `immediate` when there is no `mailbox`.** AMD's Windows driver
  does not offer `mailbox` and fell back to `fifo`; in a desktop-composited window that blocks at
  the compositor's rhythm. Same route on the RX 6900 XT, windowed: `fifo` 110 and 60 frames over
  25 ms in the first two minutes and a 1 % low of 36 fps; `immediate` 17 and 1, 1 % low of 48–53
  fps. A composited window does not tear. Borderless fullscreen stays on `fifo`.
  `AC_WINDOWED_IMMEDIATE=0` turns it off.
- **FPS-TXT-01:** the game menu no longer says that 60 FPS turns on «the 60 fps patch» (that was
  Astral Chain's). In Snap the setting caps presentation. Fixed in all four languages.
- The internal-scale help text warns that 125/150/200 % break photos today.

## Checked

- RX 6900 XT: a combined 540 s route with all of the above, exit 0, 60 fps average.
- Window with `immediate` against `fifo`: figures above.
- `AC_FPS=30`: 1801 presents per minute.
- Every adapter's tests green.

## Not checked / known problems

- **Internal scale 125/150/200 % (supersampling, TV only): broken in this version.** With 200 %
  on the RX 6900 XT, the image covers only the top-left quarter of the window, the scene another
  quarter inside it, and photos and thumbnails come out incomplete or banded. Do not use it;
  in progress as SS-02.
- Gyro with a physical controller: a test with the new sensitivity is missing.
- None of this was tested on RTX with this binary.
- Fullscreen pacing on AMD (PACE-AMD-01) and high FPS are still pending.
