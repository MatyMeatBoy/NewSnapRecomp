# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.11

**Stable line 0.9.**

## New

- **Launcher redesign:** rounded look, sidebar with a Play button, the Mejoras tab in sections (Fotos, Sombras,
  Texturas HD, Fluidez). The launcher steps aside while you play and closes when the game ends.
- **Add to Steam** with cover, logo and icon (for Big Picture and Steam Input), and a **desktop shortcut**; both open
  the game straight away with your settings.
- **VRR mode** (FreeSync / G-SYNC): one switch sets VSync and the frame limit just under your monitor's refresh
  (138 at 144 Hz). FIFO Relaxed is no longer offered (a saved one becomes VSync).
- **In-game menu:** a System tab (back to the game, restart, quit to desktop) and the whole menu in **Spanish**.
- **Shadows follow the internal resolution** by themselves (2× at 1080p, 3× at 125–150 %, 4× from 200 %); the
  shadow-resolution option is gone. The **HD shadows mode** (experimental) puts real-time shadows at their largest map
  and enlarges the level's light maps on your PC by itself after the game closes.

## Fixed

- **Pointer above 60 FPS:** the Photodex pointer moved too fast (the game handed it a fixed 1/60 s step); every pointer
  screen now uses the real frame time, averaged, so it also moves in even steps.
