# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.12

- **Anti-aliasing at any internal resolution:** AMD FSR 4 / FSR 3.1 / DLSS in **Native** quality now run on top of an
  internal resolution above 100 %: full detail plus temporal anti-aliasing, so thin lines such as window frames and
  stair edges stop shimmering. Frame generation works with it too.
- The upscaling tool used for HD shadows and texture packs runs without console windows.
- The texture pack option only shows once a pack exists; plainer wording for the HD shadows mode.
