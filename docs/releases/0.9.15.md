# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.15

- **HD shadows mode for every level, without playing first.** With «Modo Sombras HD» on, the launcher reads the
  baked shadows of all the levels from your own game files and enlarges them on your PC: the first visit to any
  level already shows them. Before, each level had to be played once and its shadows appeared on the next visit.
- **The shadows painted on stairs, decks, rocks and the ground are now part of the mode.** Besides the light maps,
  each mesh has its own texture with the sun shadows of plants and scenery; those are enlarged too, with clean
  edges where the original has stair steps and soft foliage shadows left soft.
- The build starts by itself when you save the settings with the mode on (and when the launcher opens, if levels
  are left). It can be stopped with the same button and goes on later from where it was; starting the game pauses
  it. It takes about 35 minutes on an RTX 4060 and about 6.3 GB beside the game, once.
- **In-game menu in French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese**, besides English, Spanish
  and Simplified Chinese. It follows the language asked of the game.

Not checked in this version: the HD shadows build and its look on AMD cards (it was built and compared on an
RTX 4060); the new menu languages were not reviewed by native speakers.
