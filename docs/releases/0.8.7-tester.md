# NewSnapRecomp 0.8.7-tester

**Tester build.** Replaces 0.8.6-tester.

- **HD photo thumbnails (SS-03), on by default.** When a photo is taken, Snap shrinks the pre-HUD
  scene into a 360×240 picture and encodes it to a BC1 texture; the in-level preview, the folder
  screen and the photo-selection screen draw that small texture enlarged. Now the 360×240 picture
  is drawn at 1080×720 (the photo's own size), a copy is kept for its BC1 thumbnail, and those
  screens sample the copy (`newsnap/overlays/snap_hd_photo.c`). The thumbnail is matched by format
  and size, with no per-vendor branch: block views (NVIDIA) and stand-ins (AMD RDNA 1/2) take the
  same path. Saved photos, saved thumbnails and the evaluation do not read the copy. A thumbnail
  that the game writes any other way drops its copy and shows the game's texture again.
  Launcher: Vídeo → «Miniaturas de fotos en HD».
- **Shadow resolution: «Según la resolución 3D»** (`shadow_scale = auto`): 2× at 1080p, 3× at
  125–150 %, 4× from 200 %. The option now enlarges every square depth map (Snap's three
  1024×1024 DEPTH32F maps; any other square shadow map too). Shadows painted into the scenery's
  textures do not change.

Tested on the RX 6900 XT: photo route with HD thumbnails, exit 0, 0 validation errors; the folder
screen shows each folder's correct photo, sharp. 4× shadows, 190 s, exit 0. Not tested on RTX; the
big photo inside a folder and the in-level preview are not opened by the scripted route (same
texture, same path).
