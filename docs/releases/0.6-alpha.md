# NewSnapRecomp 0.6.0-alpha

Integrates the reviewed work and continues from 0.5.1 without altering its published
artifacts. Not declared stable or physically validated on AMD.

## Changes

- PR #2 integrated with fixes: a canonical network bool, a variant read limited to shown fields
  and removal of four account overrides without a justified result/consumer. System errors are
  logged and an explicit host policy is applied without inventing a connection, accounts or
  progress.
- PR #3's session triage and motion measurement keep the privacy, segment/parity and
  inference-limit fixes.
- The exporter finds the most recent sessions even with more than 256 entries, with constant
  memory, cancellation and without deleting earlier evidence.
- IEEE CRC32 of four/eight bytes with portable slicing-by-eight tables. Keeps CRC32C,
  states/registers/results and the required CPU. Diagnostic opt-out: AC_SNAP_CRC32_SLICE8=0. A
  microbenchmark is not an FPS gain.
- Inherits 0.5.1's portable launcher/bundled kit, technical presets/tooltips, NewSnap names,
  original colour and optional presentation traces.
- A kit with an allowlist of verified SDK files, canonical public receipts and rejection of Python
  bytecode/caches. The review found and removed a cache with a local path before publishing; the
  earlier candidates are kept private.

## Checked

The final suite passes 416 tests, with one skipped for symlink privileges; CI also passes. Final
producer: `82e01064d673a0489304913575ec646da148887c`. API 3, nine capabilities. Independent
reviews of CRC, log history, applet contracts and the packaging fix.

The final ZIP/kit reviewed by inventory and checksum; install and repair of the same version keep
data. A real rebuild with the bundled kit's portable Python, with tool acquisition without
network, probes and activation correct. The frozen launcher tested with the system PATH and no
external Python.

On RTX 4060: a nine-minute route through the tutorial's photos, selection, evaluation and the
first expedition. Exit 0, no fatal; two 1080×720 JPEGs and two 240×200 thumbnails decode and pass
checksum/band audit. Original data intact. Binary and provenance detailed in the
[package acceptance](https://github.com/MatyMeatBoy/NewSnapRecomp/blob/main/docs/bugs/v06-package-acceptance.md).
The final kit also produces a runtime verified in a 60 s session that loads the post-tutorial
profile copy and continues the dialogue, without a fatal.

CRC off/on, same executable, copied caches/profiles and two reversed pairs: pooled p99
39.90→32.04 ms, median ≈16.65 ms. Both pairs lower p99; the maximum worsens in one and large
spikes remain. It is a short local route, not a promise of FPS, perfect pacing or AMD/FreeSync
behaviour. The phase after frame 600 does not show a uniform improvement either.
