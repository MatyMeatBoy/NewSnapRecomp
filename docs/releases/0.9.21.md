# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.21

- **The frame over a Pokémon, the reticle and the pointer follow every displayed frame above 60 FPS.** At 90, 120
  or 144 FPS they were placed only on the interface's 60 Hz steps, so they trailed their subject by one or two
  frames. The game's own placement now runs before each drawn frame; which subject is framed, the scoring and the
  timing of the game do not change, and at 60 FPS nothing changes.
- **Discord Rich Presence:** while the game is open your Discord profile shows "New Pokémon Snap" and the time of
  the session. A switch in «Mejoras» turns it off. Only that text is sent, to the Discord running on your PC.

Not in this version: menus and submenus drawn at the display rate (a prototype exists, not verified yet). Not
checked: Discord Rich Presence with Discord open (it was tested against a stand-in for Discord's local pipe).
