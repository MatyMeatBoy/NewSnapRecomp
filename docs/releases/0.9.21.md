# NewSnapRecomp 0.9.21

- **The frame over a Pokémon, the reticle and the pointer follow every displayed frame above 60 FPS.** At 90, 120
  or 144 FPS they were placed only on the interface's 60 Hz steps, so they trailed their subject by one or two
  frames. The game's own placement now runs before each drawn frame; which subject is framed, the scoring and the
  timing of the game do not change, and at 60 FPS nothing changes.
- **Discord Rich Presence:** while the game is open your Discord profile shows "New Pokémon Snap" and the time of
  the session. A switch in «Mejoras» turns it off. Only that text is sent, to the Discord running on your PC.

- **Smooth menus and interface (experimental, on by default):** above 60 FPS the motion of menus, submenus and
  interface elements is drawn on every displayed frame instead of 60 steps a second (one 60 Hz step of delay on
  moving elements; the logic is untouched). Switch: «Mejoras → Menús e interfaz fluidos», also in the in-game
  menu. It was run from the title to the lab at 144 FPS without ghost menus; most menu screens (pause, album,
  course select, photo evaluation) were NOT checked: if a menu looks doubled, flickers or sits out of place, turn
  it off and report it.

Not checked: Discord Rich Presence with Discord open (it was tested against a stand-in for Discord's local pipe).
